TYPESLASHER 1.0 — STATIC WEB RELEASE

Serve this folder through HTTP or HTTPS, or upload its contents to a static web
host. index.html is the game; assets.html is the 3D food review studio.
Do not open index.html directly with a file:// URL.

Use a desktop/laptop with a physical QWERTY keyboard. Start with Arcade, Sprout,
and Relaxed pace. Escape pauses. Sound & feel controls music, volume, motion,
graphics, and Beat Kitchen tap calibration.

All fonts and food models are local. No login, analytics, or AI service is used.
Progress saves on the current browser and site address. Use the same address
each time to keep the same notebook. Clearing site storage clears progress.

Food models, music sequence and effects were created for Typeslasher.
Three.js license: assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt
Outfit font license: fonts/outfit-OFL.txt
DM Mono font license: fonts/dm-mono-OFL.txt

Validated with Chromium on Windows. Firefox, Safari, other devices and physical
audio setups need playtesting. There is no offline service worker in this release.
